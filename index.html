About Us

A professional law firm based in Nicosia, Cyprus, providing targeted legal advice to individuals, families and businesses with an emphasis on confidentiality, careful analysis and practical judgment.

Founder and Advocate Panayiota Georgiou brings over a decade of experience across corporate law, compliance, family law and international trade. She is admitted to the Cyprus Bar, holds an LL.B. and an LL.M. in International Trade Law, and is a certified Compliance Officer (ICA) and Registered Family Law Mediator.
